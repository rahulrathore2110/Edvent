Tally Prime — Complete In-Depth Master Syllabus

This syllabus takes you from beginner to advanced level in Tally Prime, covering company setup, accounting, inventory, GST, TDS/TCS, banking, payroll, cost centres, financial reports, security, and practical business operations.

It is structured for learners in India who want to use Tally Prime in accounting jobs, office administration, bookkeeping, taxation support, or business management.

Tally Prime releases differ in their menus and available features, so this syllabus covers the major standard workflows and advanced capabilities rather than assuming every command appears in every installation.

Part 1 — Tally Prime fundamentals

1. Introduction and interface

Understand what Tally Prime is and where it is used.

Understand accounting software versus spreadsheets.

Explore the Gateway of Tally and main navigation.

Learn the top menu, buttons, action bar and calculator panel.

Understand vouchers, masters, reports and configuration.

Learn keyboard shortcuts and function-key commands.

Use search and Go To to access reports and features.

Understand the difference between company data and configuration.

Learn how to exit safely and handle confirmation prompts.

2. Company creation and management

Create a new company.

Enter company name, address, contact details and country.

Set the financial year and books beginning date.

Configure base currency and decimal places.

Enable accounting, inventory and statutory features as required.

Set the GST registration type and registration details where applicable.

Alter company details.

Select, shut and switch between companies.

Create multiple companies for different businesses.

Understand group companies and consolidated reporting where supported.

Set company-specific features and configurations.

Understand data locations and company data directories.

3. Data management

Create and organize company data.

Back up company data.

Restore a backup and verify the restored company.

Split company data at the start of a new financial year where appropriate.

Understand data migration between releases.

Export and import company data using supported formats.

Understand synchronization and remote access where licensed.

Protect backups and sensitive financial information.

Test backup restoration before relying on a backup.

Part 8 — Tally Prime keyboard shortcuts

Learn these alongside the relevant modules. Shortcuts and their availability may vary by release and screen.

Shortcut Common purpose
F2 Change date in supported screens
Alt + F2 Change period in supported reports
F3 Select company in supported screens
F4 Contra voucher in applicable voucher-entry screens
F5 Payment voucher
F6 Receipt voucher
F7 Journal voucher
F8 Sales voucher
F9 Purchase voucher
Alt + F5 Debit Note in applicable screens
Alt + F6 Credit Note in applicable screens
Alt + C Create a master from a supported selection field
Ctrl + A Accept or save in many applicable screens
Esc Exit the current screen or cancel the current operation
Alt + G Open Go To in supported releases
Ctrl + Q Quit or exit in supported contexts

Note: These are common Tally Prime shortcuts, not a guarantee that each one works in every screen or customized configuration. Learn the shortcut hints displayed by your own installation.
