Microsoft PowerPoint — Complete In-Depth Master Syllabus

This syllabus takes you from beginner to advanced PowerPoint user. It covers the Ribbon tabs, their major command groups, presentation design, animations, transitions, Slide Master, multimedia, presenting tools, collaboration, accessibility, and professional presentation production.

The syllabus primarily follows the desktop version of PowerPoint for Windows with Microsoft 365. PowerPoint for Mac, the web version, and older editions may have different menus or feature availability.

Complete learning roadmap

File

Create, save, share, export and print presentations

Home

Slides, layouts, text, paragraphs, drawing and editing

Insert

Tables, images, shapes, charts, audio, video and links

Design

Themes, variants, slide size and backgrounds

Transitions

Slide transitions, effects and timing

Animations

Object animations, motion paths and sequencing

Slide Show

Presenting, Presenter View, rehearsal and recording

Record

Narration, camera, timings and recorded presentations

Review

Proofing, comments, comparison and collaboration

View

Slide views, guides, masters, zoom and windows

Advanced

Slide Master, templates, media, automation and design systems

Professional Projects

Business decks, training presentations, reports and pitch decks

Important: PowerPoint mastery requires more than memorizing commands. You should be able to structure a presentation, communicate a message visually, maintain consistent formatting, build reusable templates, deliver a presentation confidently, and export it reliably.
